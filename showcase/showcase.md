# Fathom

You are a small light in an enormous dark. Swim the flooded corridors of a deep-sea maze, grazing the luminous plankton that marks your way. Clear every mote and descend, taking your score and your remaining lives deeper into the abyss.

Every meal makes your glow stronger and your window into the maze wider. Paths you have explored linger as faint memories inside that circle; beyond it, the sea swallows them again. Send a sonar pulse around a corner to find the next passage, then decide whether the knowledge was worth the noise.

Three hunters answer different signals. Lanternjaw follows your light beneath an amber bell that looks just like a harmless drifter. Eyeless Gloamfin listens, chasing the place a sound betrayed you and casting violet pings of its own. Flarefish blooms in the darkness, illuminating rock and water in a sudden warm flash.

Keep moving, listen for the next sweep, and leave a cloud of ink when a light hunter closes in. Getting caught costs a reserve life, but your eaten plankton and remembered paths remain. The next dive starts with another chance to turn a little light into a way through.
